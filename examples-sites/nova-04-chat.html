Three cohorts explain 78 percent of the Q3 churn, and only one of them is a pricing problem.

  • Enterprise, signed before seat minimums (Jan-Feb 2025): 9 accounts downgraded at renewal, $612k ARR at risk. They were sold unlimited seats and are now billed per seat.
  • Growth, annual with a legacy 25 percent discount: 14 accounts did not renew, $318k. Most cited the price after the discount lapsed.
  • Starter, card failures: 41 accounts, $214k. Recovery is mostly a dunning problem, not a churn problem.

Fix order: dunning first, because it is the cheapest and recovers in under two weeks. Then the legacy discount cohort, where a documented migration path keeps most of the ARR. The seat minimum cohort is a contract change and needs legal plus finance.
